About Me

Objective

I am a BS Information Technology graduate majoring in Database Systems with nearly 6 years of experience as a Full Stack Developer. Detail-oriented and driven, I am seeking a challenging role to leverage my expertise in Web and Software Development/Engineering, database optimization, AI-powered automation, web scraping, and scalable systems—with the goal of contributing to a forward-thinking team that values innovation and growth. My strongest specialization is in PHP and Laravel development, supported by deep knowledge in database optimization, server tuning, API performance, and building scalable web applications. One of my most notable achievements was being among the pioneering developers of a POS System in the Philippines, successfully deployed to numerous clients nationwide. Although bound by NDA, I can proudly say I developed the admin backend and POS system entirely from scratch—designing both the backend and frontend without templates, ensuring a fully customized solution. I have worked both as an independent developer and as part of collaborative, dynamic teams, including 3 years as a remote developer. Additionally, I’ve served as a part-time tutor and mentor, helping university students with their capstone system projects. Passionate about continuous growth, I’ve built multiple end-to-end web applications—from data analysis, algorithm design, database architecture, UI/UX, backend, to frontend development. I also have hands-on experience in machine learning and embedded systems, such as developing an intruder detection system using Raspberry Pi and OpenCV, which automatically captures and stores images/videos when detecting motion in restricted areas for a local government project. I am a dedicated, hardworking, and innovative developer who thrives on solving complex challenges and delivering high-quality, impactful solutions.
