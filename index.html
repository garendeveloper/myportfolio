About

A developer who loves databases.

I finished BS Information Technology, majoring in Database Systems. I have worked alone, in teams, and remotely. I like handling a feature from the database all the way to the screen.

I was one of the first developers of a POS system in the Philippines. Many clients used it. I built the admin backend and the POS myself, without templates. It is under NDA, so I can talk about it but I can't show it.

Right now I work on SocialValet. It helps people plan, post and track their social media content, with some AI tools. I also help students with their capstone projects. On the side, I made a few camera and Raspberry Pi projects.

What I do best

  • Connecting apps together: login with OAuth, webhooks, and posting to WordPress, Instagram, Facebook, Pinterest and TikTok.
  • Background jobs: queues and schedulers that keep working even when there is a lot to do.
  • AI in real apps: writing content with Anthropic, OpenAI and Google models.
  • Fast databases: good table design, indexes and faster queries.
